Introducing Fabbrica

A fabrication platform for
making, selling, and
creating together.

Industrial craft, made for the world.

Fabbrica is a platform where finished products find their people, and a co-creation engine where makers and streamers dream up what's next, live and together. Fabbrica also offers makers and their community a command center for managing factories and manufacturing — milestones, communication, samples, documents, shipments all in one place.

How it works

From a stream to a product.

Share your brief

Send your concept, specs, or franchise requirements — or bring an idea born in a co-creation gathering. We scope the project and open your portal workspace within days.

We build your pipeline

Design, tooling, and production milestones appear in your dashboard — each with owners, dates, and deliverables.

Make, sell, repeat

Approve samples, watch shipments move, then list the finished product on the marketplace — or gather the community to co-create the next one, live. From a stream to a SKU.
